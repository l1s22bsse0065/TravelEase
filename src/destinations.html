Top Mountain Destinations

Discover the breathtaking beauty of Northern Pakistan.

Gilgit-Baltistan

Hunza

Known for stunning forts, kind people, and clean streets.

  • Attractions: Altit Fort, Attabad Lake
  • Best Time: April – October
  • Food: Chapshuro, Mamtu
  • Travel Cost: PKR 15,000 – 25,000

Skardu

Base camp for adventure trekkers and serene lakes.

  • Attractions: Shigar Fort, Kachura Lake
  • Best Time: May – September
  • Food: Yak meat, buckwheat bread
  • Travel Cost: PKR 18,000 – 30,000

Fairy Meadows

Beautiful camping spot at the base of Nanga Parbat.

  • Attractions: Nanga Parbat view, hiking
  • Best Time: June – September
  • Food: Local trout, lentil soup
  • Travel Cost: PKR 12,000 – 20,000

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Swat

The Switzerland of Pakistan with emerald rivers and lush valleys.

  • Attractions: Fizagat Park, Swat Museum
  • Best Time: March – September
  • Food: Trout, Chapli kebab
  • Travel Cost: PKR 10,000 – 18,000

Kalam

Cool climate, pine forests, and gushing waterfalls.

  • Attractions: Mahodand Lake, Ushu Forest
  • Best Time: April – August
  • Food: Barbecue, local bread
  • Travel Cost: PKR 12,000 – 20,000

Naran & Kaghan

Stunning alpine scenery and the legendary Saif-ul-Malook lake.

  • Attractions: Lake Saif-ul-Malook, Babusar Top
  • Best Time: May – August
  • Food: Fried fish, karahi
  • Travel Cost: PKR 10,000 – 22,000
